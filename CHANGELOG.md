# Changelog

## v0.1.1-alpha

The first version of ReRAC2. The game boots to its main menu, and New Game starts on Aranos, the first planet.

- Aranos's ship is lit as in the original: its walls, floors, props and lights.
- Enemies flash red and hop back when you hit them, and blow apart when they die.
- The nanotech meter lines up and pulses as in the original.
- Fullscreen and the render resolution can be changed while you play.
